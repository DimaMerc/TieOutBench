## (a) Objective and daily reset
Section: Fund Summary, Investment Objective
> The Fund seeks daily investment results that, before fees and expenses, correspond to approximately two times (2x) the daily total return of the US Cannabis ETF. The Fund does not seek to achieve its stated investment objective over a period of time greater than a single day.

Section: Principal Investment Strategies
> A single day is measured from the time the Fund calculates its net asset value ("NAV") to the time of the Fund's next NAV calculation.

Section: Principal Risks, Correlation Risk
> In order to achieve a high degree of correlation with the US Cannabis ETF, the Fund seeks to rebalance its portfolio daily to keep exposure consistent with its investment objective.

Section: More Information About the Fund's Principal Investment Strategies
> The Fund will reposition its portfolio at the end of every trading day. Therefore, if an investor purchases Fund shares at close of the markets on a given trading day, the investor's exposure to the US Cannabis ETF would reflect approximately 200% of the performance of the US Cannabis ETF during the following trading day, subject to the charges and expenses noted above.

> The Advisor will attempt to position the Fund's portfolio to ensure that the Fund does not gain or lose more than 90% of its NAV on a given day.

## (b) Use of swaps; counterparties
Section: Principal Investment Strategies
> The Fund is an actively managed ETF that seeks to provide investment results that are approximately two times (2x) the daily total return, before fees and expenses, of the US Cannabis ETF, an affiliated ETF, by entering into one or more swap agreements on the US Cannabis ETF.

> The Fund expects that cash balances in connection with the use of such financial instruments ("Collateral") will typically be held in money market instruments, including money market funds, or other cash equivalents.

Section: More Information ... Principal Investment Strategies
> The Fund will enter into swap agreements to pursue its investment objective of delivering approximately 200% return to its underlying ETF for a single day. Swap agreements are contracts entered into primarily with major financial institutions for a specified period ranging from a day to more than one year. [...] The gross return to be exchanged or "swapped" between the parties is calculated with respect to a "notional amount"

> the terms of a swap agreement between the Fund and its counterparty may permit the counterparty to immediately close out the transaction with the Fund.

Footnote (b):
> The cost of investing in swaps, including the embedded cost of the swap and the operating expenses of the referenced assets, is an indirect expense that is not included in the above fee table and is not reflected in the expense example below. The total indirect cost of investing in swaps, including the embedded cost of the swap and the operating expenses of the referenced assets, was 6.96% for the most recent fiscal year, which may be partially offset by interest earned on cash collateral balances in connection with the swaps.

Section: More Information ... Strategies, "The Effects of Fees and Expenses on the Return of the Fund for a Single Day"
> the expected return of the Fund over one trading day is equal to the gross expected return, which is the daily US Cannabis ETF's return multiplied by the Fund's daily leveraged investment objective, minus (i) financing charges incurred by the portfolio and (ii) daily operating expenses.

## (f) Creation and redemption
Section: More Information About the Trust and the Fund
> Creation Units of the Fund are issued and redeemed principally in exchange for a deposit of cash totaling the NAV of the Creation Units.

Section: Principal Risks, Cash Transaction Risk
> the Fund intends to effect redemptions for cash. [...] Further, effecting purchases and redemptions primarily in cash may cause the Fund to incur certain costs, such as portfolio transaction costs. These costs can decrease the Fund's NAV if not offset by an authorized participant transaction fee.

Section: Shareholder Information, Active Investors and Market Timing
> The Fund also imposes transaction fees on such Creation Unit transactions that are designed to offset the Fund's transfer and other transaction costs associated with the issuance and redemption of the Creation Unit shares.

## Other lines useful for the gap table
Section: Calculating Net Asset Value
> The Fund calculates NAV by (i) taking the current market value of its total assets, (ii) subtracting any liabilities, and (iii) dividing that amount by the total number of shares owned by shareholders.
> The Fund calculates NAV once each business day as of the regularly scheduled close of normal trading on the NYSE (normally 4:00 p.m. Eastern Time). The NYSE is typically closed on weekends and most national holidays.
