DRIVER POST for the cross-vendor article (feed post; publish runbook at bottom)
===========================================================================

One of the models in my study scored 0.322 and tripped six separate auto-fail gates on a valuation task.

Given a bigger token budget, the same model on the same task scored 0.951 and tripped nothing.

The difference wasn't the model. It was me.

I'd spent six weeks publishing evals where every frontier model came from one vendor - and saying so, in every article, because a benchmark that has only met one family of models hasn't really been tested. This summer I finally added two more vendors. Eight models, three companies, same gold cases, same rubrics.

The harness broke three times before it produced a single comparable number.

The dangerous one: modern models "think" before answering, and vendors disagree about whether that thinking comes out of your answer budget. On one endpoint it's free. On another it isn't. So a budget that was generous for every model I'd tested was quietly starving the new ones on my longest case - and a starved model doesn't throw an error. It just gives you a worse answer. Which is exactly what you're looking for when you benchmark, so you write it down and move on.

Four scores. All plausible. All wrong.

The rule I'd offer anyone comparing models to decide what to deploy: before you ask which model won, prove your harness gave them the same test. Mine didn't. Twice. And I built it.

Article in the first comment - including what survived the fix, and a cheap, fast model that landed a valuation one cent from the gold answer.
