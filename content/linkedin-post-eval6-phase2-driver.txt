I gave eight AI models the tools a desk uses for a corporate action. Then I added a second AI to check the work.

With tools, more runs passed every check: 35 of 48, up from 32.

With the checker, fewer did: 27.

The checker looked up the position and recalculated. Then it rejected 20 of the 46 bookings my grader scores as correct, and each time the first model changed its work.

Not every objection was wrong. Five were about a payment date that no document states. My test required that date, so part of this result comes from my environment, not from the AI.

The 90-second video shows the recorded calls. The article has the rest: what a stronger checker did, and what I would give the checker next time. Links in the first comment.
