IMAGE PROMPTS for the retrospective post (content/linkedin-post-400-runs-driver.txt)
Drafted 2026-10-27. He generates the picture himself; these are the prompts. Portrait 4:5 (1080x1350) gets the most
feed space. Every prompt asks for NO legible text: image generators garble numbers, and every figure we publish must
trace to a run record. If the post's line should appear on the picture, overlay it afterwards (see OVERLAY).
===========================================================================

WHY THESE SCENES
- The post's point is one line on a statement, checked by a person. A red pen on one printed line carries it without words.
- Prompt A is the four-eyes principle (Vier-Augen-Prinzip), the name every DACH reader has for a maker-checker control,
  and the post's checker finding in one picture.
- The places are where the readers are: Frankfurt, Zurich, Boston; Singapore for the "exotic" variant.
- Editorial-photography wording ("candid, not posed", "natural skin texture", "no stock-photo smile") is what keeps it
  from reading as a stock render.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT A - four eyes, Frankfurt at dawn (recommended)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Editorial photograph, candid, not posed. A glass-walled meeting room on a high floor at dawn, the Frankfurt skyline
and the river soft in the background. Two professionals at a pale oak table: a woman in her late thirties in a
tailored navy suit, hair tied back, composed and attentive, and a man in his forties in shirt sleeves. Between them a
single printed settlement statement; she holds a red pen and points at one line, he leans in to read it. A laptop
open beside them shows a blurred spreadsheet. Warm low morning light from the left, cool blue window light behind,
shallow depth of field, 50mm lens, natural skin texture, muted palette of navy, cream and oak. No legible text, no
logos, no watermark. Portrait, 4:5 aspect ratio.

Negative / avoid: text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, extra fingers, plastic skin, stock-photo smile, lens flare,
neon, crypto imagery.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT B - the checker at sunrise, Lake Zurich
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Editorial photograph, candid. A hotel terrace above Lake Zurich at sunrise, the Alps faint in the haze, mist on the
water. A woman in her thirties in a cream linen suit sits at a small table with an espresso, an open laptop and a
printed trade confirmation; she is circling one figure with a red pen, reading glasses pushed up into her hair, calm
concentration. Golden side light, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural skin, no retouching look, quiet colours.
No legible text, no logos, no watermark. Portrait, 4:5 aspect ratio.

Negative / avoid: text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, extra fingers, plastic skin, posed smile, oversaturation.

TOOL NOTES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Midjourney: append "--ar 4:5 --style raw" and put the avoid-list after "--no" (e.g. "--no text, logos, watermark").
- GPT image / Ideogram / Flux: paste the prompt as written; set the size to portrait 1080x1350 or 4:5.
- Run three or four variations and pick the one with the most natural hands and face; discard any with text artefacts.
- No real firm's floor, badge or logo should be recognisable (the generator will sometimes invent one; discard those).

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERLAY (optional, on the finished picture)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
If the post's line should be on the image, do not ask the generator for it. Send the file and a small script adds a
band at the bottom in the series' ledger palette, like the eval-8 card: "Strong on the call, shaky on the number. I
expected the opposite." and "TieOutBench  -  evals.finance". The text is ours, so it stays true.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
FIRST COMMENT ADDITION (only if the picture is AI-generated)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Add as the last line of the first comment: "The picture is AI-generated. The numbers are not."
It pre-empts the one objection a reader of this post will make, and it is in his voice (plain, wry).
