MAIN (~100 words):
On the first question: the analyst, for a practical reason. In my own evaluation work the hard part is never the tooling. It is writing down what correct work looks like, step by step: which source governs, which number has to tie to which. Only the domain expert can write that, and once it is written, the quants and the models both have something to be measured against. On the third: the models I test get credit for saying a figure cannot be determined from the documents. The ones tuned for confidence guess instead. I suspect the same trade-off holds for analysts.
