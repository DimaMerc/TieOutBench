Can AI replace a financial analyst?

I built TieOutBench to test the work we would hand one.

In one valuation test, a model calculated a discount rate of 12.62% and entered 12.31% as its final answer. In a corporate-actions test, a model found the corrected dividend notice and still proposed booking $8,500 when the correct amount was $6,800.

Other results were much stronger. One model passed all six corporate-actions cases with nothing to flag. The interesting part is seeing where the work holds up and where it still needs checking.

This 90-second video walks through the evidence.
